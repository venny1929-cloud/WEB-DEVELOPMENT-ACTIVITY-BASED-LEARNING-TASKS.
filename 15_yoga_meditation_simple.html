Welcome to Serene Space

Simple yoga and meditation ideas for everyday wellbeing.

Basic Yoga Poses

Mountain Pose

Stand tall, relax your shoulders and breathe comfortably.

Easy Sitting Pose

Sit comfortably, keep your back relaxed and focus on breathing.

Tree Pose

Practise balance with wall support if needed. Stop if uncomfortable.

Meditation Techniques

Breathing Meditation

Sit comfortably and notice your natural breathing for 2–5 minutes.

Sound Meditation

Listen to gentle sounds and calmly bring your attention back when distracted.

Mindful Pause

Notice your surroundings and how your body feels in the present moment.
