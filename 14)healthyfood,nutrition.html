Easy Healthy Recipes

Simple meal inspiration with ingredients and approximate nutrition information.

Rainbow Vegetable Salad

A crisp mix of vegetables with lemon dressing.

⏱ 15 minutes · Approx. 220 kcal/serving

Ingredients
  • Cucumber and tomato
  • Carrot and leafy greens
  • Lemon juice and a little olive oil

Fruit Oat Bowl

A comforting breakfast with fruit and oats.

⏱ 10 minutes · Approx. 300 kcal/serving

Ingredients
  • Rolled oats and milk
  • Banana or seasonal fruit
  • Nuts or seeds, if desired

Lentil Vegetable Soup

A warm, satisfying dish with lentils and vegetables.

⏱ 30 minutes · Approx. 260 kcal/serving

Ingredients
  • Cooked lentils or dal
  • Carrot, tomato and onion
  • Garlic, spices and water

Healthy Eating Tips

Simple habits that can support everyday wellbeing.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drink water regularly and more when you are thirsty or active.

🌈 Add Variety

Try different coloured vegetables, fruits, grains and protein foods.

🍽️ Eat Mindfully

Take your time, enjoy meals and notice your hunger and fullness.
